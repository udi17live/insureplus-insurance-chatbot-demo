# Life Insurance FAQ

> Answers to the most common questions about Insure Plus life insurance.

## Do I need a medical exam to get life insurance?

No. Insure Plus life insurance does not require a medical examination or GP report at the point of purchase. You only need to provide your date of birth and smoking status. Medical records may be reviewed at the point of a claim.

## What happens if I stop paying my premium?

If a direct debit payment fails and is not resolved within 30 days, the policy lapses. A lapsed policy provides no coverage and cannot be reinstated. You would need to purchase a new policy, likely at a higher premium due to your older age.

## Can I increase my sum assured later?

No. The sum assured is fixed at the time of purchase. To increase your coverage, you would need to purchase a new policy. The premium for the new policy would be based on your age at the time of the new purchase.

## Is the payout taxable?

The life insurance payout is paid tax-free to the named beneficiary. If the policy is written into trust, it may also fall outside of your estate for inheritance tax purposes. Consult a financial advisor for estate planning advice.

## Can I have more than one life insurance policy?

Yes. You can hold multiple life insurance policies with different providers. Insure Plus allows one active life policy per user on the platform. Multiple policies are independent and each pays out separately on a valid claim.
