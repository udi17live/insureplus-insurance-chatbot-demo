# Device Accidental Damage and Theft Cover

> Insure Plus device insurance covers sudden accidental damage and theft — understanding what qualifies helps you make a successful claim.

## What Counts as Accidental Damage

Accidental damage means physical damage caused by an unexpected and unintended event. Covered examples include a cracked screen from a drop, liquid damage from a spill, and physical breakage from an impact. The damage must be sudden, not the result of gradual wear.

## What Counts as Theft

Theft is covered when your device is taken from you by force or without your knowledge. Pickpocketing, bag snatching, and burglary are covered. For theft from a vehicle, the device must have been stored out of sight and the vehicle must show signs of forced entry.

## Repair vs Replacement

If the device can be repaired by an authorised repairer, the claim will cover repair costs up to the declared value. If repair is not economical or not possible, the device will be replaced with one of equivalent specification and age. Cash settlements are not provided.

## Overseas Coverage

Accidental damage and theft claims are covered while you are travelling outside the UK. The same claim limits apply. Claims must be reported within 30 days of the incident.

## Excess on Claims

Each device insurance claim is subject to a £50 excess. This means if your repair costs £180, Insure Plus pays £130 and you pay £50. The excess applies per claim, not per policy year.
