# Life Insurance Beneficiary Guide

> Your beneficiary is the person who receives the life insurance payout — choosing and keeping this up to date is one of the most important steps when purchasing life cover.

## What a Beneficiary Is

A beneficiary is the person or persons named to receive the sum assured when a valid claim is made. You can name one or more beneficiaries. If multiple beneficiaries are named, the sum assured is split equally between them unless you specify different proportions.

## Who Can Be a Beneficiary

Any person can be named as a beneficiary — a spouse, partner, child, parent, or friend. A beneficiary does not need to be a family member. You can also name a trust or estate as the beneficiary.

## How to Name a Beneficiary

During the quote and purchase process the assistant will ask for your beneficiary's full name. This is stored against your policy record. You can update your beneficiary after purchase by contacting Insure Plus support.

## What Happens at Claim

When a claim is made, the beneficiary contacts Insure Plus support with the policy number and a certified copy of the death certificate. A claims handler is assigned and the payout is made within 10 business days of claim approval directly to the beneficiary.

## Keeping Beneficiary Details Current

It is important to review your beneficiary details if your circumstances change — for example after marriage, divorce, or the death of a named beneficiary. An outdated beneficiary nomination can delay or complicate the claims process.
