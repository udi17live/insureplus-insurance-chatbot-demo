# Motor Insurance Overview

> Insure Plus motor insurance covers your car against damage, theft, and third-party liability, with three coverage tiers to suit every driver.

## What Motor Insurance Covers

Motor insurance protects you financially if your car is involved in an accident, stolen, or damaged. Third-party only covers injury or damage you cause to others. Third-party fire and theft adds protection if your car is stolen or damaged by fire. Comprehensive cover also protects your own car against accidental damage, regardless of fault.

## Who Can Get Motor Insurance

Insure Plus motor insurance is available to drivers aged 18 and over with a valid UK driving licence. The vehicle must be registered in the UK. Classic vehicles over 25 years old and commercial vehicles are not covered under this product.

## Coverage Tiers

There are three coverage tiers. Third-party only is the minimum legal requirement. Third-party fire and theft adds fire and theft protection. Comprehensive is the highest tier and covers accidental damage to your own vehicle in addition to all third-party and fire and theft protections.

## Policy Duration and Renewal

All motor insurance policies run for 12 months from the purchase date. A renewal reminder is sent 30 days before expiry. You can renew through the assistant using your existing details. Premiums may change at renewal based on your claims history and updated vehicle information.

## Getting a Motor Quote

To quote motor insurance, the assistant needs your vehicle registration, make, manufacture year, date of birth, annual mileage, preferred coverage type, and no-claims discount years. The assistant will ask for these one at a time before presenting a quote.
