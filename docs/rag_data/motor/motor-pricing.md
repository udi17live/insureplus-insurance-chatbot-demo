# Motor Insurance Pricing

> Insure Plus motor insurance uses fixed annual premiums based on coverage tier, with adjustments for driver age and no-claims discount years.

## Base Annual Premiums by Coverage Tier

All motor policies start from the following fixed base rates before adjustments are applied.

Third-party only: £280 per year.
Third-party fire and theft: £380 per year.
Comprehensive: £520 per year.

These base rates apply to a standard risk profile: driver aged 30–39, annual mileage under 10,000, and zero NCD years.

## Age Adjustments

Drivers aged 18–24 have a loading of £150 added to the base premium. Drivers aged 25–29 have a loading of £60. Drivers aged 30–39 pay the base rate with no loading. Drivers aged 40 and over receive a £30 reduction.

## No-Claims Discount Adjustments

Each NCD year reduces the premium by £40. One NCD year saves £40. Two NCD years save £80. Three NCD years save £120. Four NCD years save £160. Five or more NCD years save £200.

## Mileage Adjustments

Annual mileage under 5,000 miles: £30 reduction. Mileage between 5,000 and 10,000: no adjustment. Mileage between 10,000 and 20,000: £50 addition. Mileage over 20,000: £100 addition.

## Example Quotes

A 28-year-old driver with zero NCD, 8,000 miles annually, choosing comprehensive cover: £520 + £60 = £580 per year (£48.33 per month). A 35-year-old driver with 3 NCD years, 6,000 miles, choosing third-party fire and theft: £380 − £120 = £260 per year (£21.67 per month).
