# Bike Insurance Exclusions

> Insure Plus bike insurance does not cover certain situations — these exclusions apply regardless of coverage tier.

## Riding Without the Correct Licence

Claims will not be paid if the rider did not hold a valid licence appropriate for the engine size of the bike at the time of the incident. For example, an AM licence holder riding a 600cc motorcycle is not covered.

## Riding Under the Influence

Any claim arising from an incident where the rider was under the influence of alcohol or controlled drugs is excluded. This exclusion applies to all coverage tiers.

## Track and Competition Use

Bike insurance covers road use only. Any incident occurring on a race track, closed circuit, or during a timed competition event is excluded. This includes organised track days.

## Modifications Not Declared

Performance or structural modifications that were not declared at the time of purchase are excluded. If you modify your bike after purchase, you must notify Insure Plus. Undeclared modifications may void your policy.

## Theft Without Evidence of Forced Entry

If your bike is stolen without evidence of forced entry or without the use of an approved lock, the theft claim may be declined. Bikes must be secured with a ground anchor or approved disc lock when parked overnight.

## Wear and Tear

Gradual mechanical deterioration, corrosion, and tyre wear are not covered. Bike insurance covers sudden unforeseen events only.
