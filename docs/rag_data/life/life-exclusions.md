# Life Insurance Exclusions

> Insure Plus life insurance does not pay out in certain circumstances — knowing these exclusions ensures you understand when a claim will be declined.

## Pre-Existing Conditions

If death or a critical illness diagnosis is directly caused by a condition you had before purchasing the policy and did not declare, the claim will be declined. You are not asked to disclose medical history during quoting, but a claim investigation will review your medical records.

## Suicide Within the First 12 Months

Claims arising from suicide or intentional self-harm within the first 12 months of the policy start date will not be paid. After 12 months, the full sum assured is payable regardless of cause of death.

## Death Resulting from Criminal Activity

If the policyholder dies as a direct result of committing or attempting to commit a criminal offence, the claim will be declined.

## Misrepresentation of Smoking Status

Declaring non-smoker status when you are a smoker is considered material misrepresentation and will void the policy. All claims are subject to review, and smoking status may be verified through medical records.

## Survival Period for Critical Illness

For critical illness claims, the policyholder must survive for at least 14 days following the diagnosis date. If death occurs within 14 days of the diagnosis, the critical illness benefit is not paid. The standard life benefit would apply instead.
