# Motor Claims Process

> To make a motor insurance claim, contact Insure Plus support as soon as possible after the incident with your policy number and incident details.

## When to Make a Claim

You should make a claim if your vehicle has been damaged in an accident, stolen, or damaged by fire. You should also claim if you caused damage or injury to a third party. For minor incidents where repair costs are below your excess, it may not be worth claiming as it could affect your NCD.

## Information You Will Need

When making a claim you will need your policy number, the date and location of the incident, a description of what happened, contact details of any other parties involved, and photographs of the damage where possible. For theft, you will also need a police crime reference number.

## How to Submit a Claim

Claims are submitted by contacting Insure Plus support by phone or email. The assistant cannot process claims — it will provide the support contact details if you ask. A claims handler will be assigned and will contact you within one business day.

## Claim Assessment and Settlement

Your claims handler will assess the incident, request any additional documentation, and arrange for a vehicle inspection if required. For total loss, the settlement is based on the market value of the vehicle at the time of the incident.

## Impact on Your Premium

Making a claim resets your NCD to zero at renewal unless you have protected NCD cover. Your premium at renewal may increase. Claims are recorded for five years.
