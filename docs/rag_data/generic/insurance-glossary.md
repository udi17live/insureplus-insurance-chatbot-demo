# Insurance Glossary

> Definitions of common insurance terms used across Insure Plus products.

## Premium

The premium is the amount you pay for your insurance policy. On Insure Plus, premiums are quoted annually but can be viewed as a monthly figure. The premium is calculated based on the details you provide during the quote process. A higher risk profile results in a higher premium.

## Excess

The excess is the amount you agree to pay toward a claim before your insurer covers the rest. For example, if your excess is £200 and your claim is £800, you pay £200 and the insurer pays £600. A higher voluntary excess usually reduces your premium.

## No-Claims Discount (NCD)

A no-claims discount is a reduction applied to your motor insurance premium for each year you have not made a claim. On Insure Plus, NCD years range from 0 to 5 or more. The more NCD years you have, the lower your premium.

## Coverage Type

Coverage type defines what your policy protects against. For motor and bike insurance, the options are third-party only, third-party fire and theft, and comprehensive. For life insurance, coverage type relates to the sum assured and optional add-ons such as critical illness cover.

## Policy Period

The policy period is the duration your insurance is active. All Insure Plus policies run for 12 months from the date of purchase. Renewal reminders are sent 30 days before expiry.

## Sum Assured

The sum assured is the fixed amount paid out by a life insurance policy upon a valid claim. On Insure Plus, life insurance sum assured options range from £50,000 to £500,000.
