# Motor Insurance Add-Ons

> Insure Plus motor insurance offers optional add-ons that extend your coverage beyond the standard policy.

## Breakdown Cover

Breakdown cover provides roadside assistance if your vehicle breaks down. A recovery vehicle will attend and either repair the vehicle on the spot or tow it to the nearest garage. This add-on costs £60 per year and is available on all coverage tiers.

## Protected No-Claims Discount

Protected NCD allows you to make one claim per policy year without losing your accumulated no-claims discount. This add-on costs £45 per year and is only available to drivers with two or more NCD years. Without protection, any claim resets your NCD to zero.

## Legal Expenses Cover

Legal expenses cover funds the cost of pursuing an uninsured driver or recovering uninsured losses after a non-fault accident. It provides up to £50,000 in legal costs. This add-on costs £30 per year.

## Key Cover

Key cover replaces lost, stolen, or damaged vehicle keys and remote fobs. It also covers locksmith call-out charges if you are locked out. This add-on costs £20 per year.

## How to Add Extras

Add-ons are offered during the quote confirmation step. The assistant will present available add-ons after you confirm your core coverage selection. Add-on costs are added to your annual premium before the final quote is generated.
