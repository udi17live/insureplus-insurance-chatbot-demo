# Device Insurance Exclusions

> Device insurance does not cover all situations — these exclusions apply to all device policies on Insure Plus.

## Intentional Damage

Damage that was deliberately caused by the policyholder or a person acting with their knowledge is not covered. This includes devices damaged to make a fraudulent claim.

## Theft Without a Police Report

Theft claims require a police crime reference number. If theft is not reported to the police, the claim will be declined. You must report the theft to the police within 24 hours of discovery and to Insure Plus within 48 hours.

## Unexplained Loss or Disappearance

If your device simply goes missing and there is no evidence of theft or accidental damage, the claim will not be paid. Loss without a traceable cause is excluded from all device insurance policies.

## Software Faults and Viruses

Software issues, operating system failures, viruses, and malware damage are not covered. Device insurance covers physical damage only. Software problems are the responsibility of the device manufacturer or the user.

## Devices Over 3 Years Old

Devices that were more than 3 years old at the time the policy was purchased are not eligible. If a device exceeds 3 years of age during the policy term, it remains covered until renewal but cannot be re-insured thereafter.

## Cosmetic Damage Only

Scratches, dents, and other cosmetic damage that does not affect the functionality of the device are not covered. Claims must relate to damage that impairs the device's normal operation.
