# Device Insurance FAQ

> Answers to the most common questions about Insure Plus device insurance.

## Can I insure a second-hand device?

Yes. Second-hand devices are eligible as long as they are less than 3 years old and in full working condition at the time of purchase. The declared value must reflect the current market value of the device, not the original purchase price.

## What if my device is repaired but the same fault recurs?

If the same fault recurs within 90 days of a repair covered by a previous claim, it is treated as a continuation of the original claim and no additional excess is charged. After 90 days, a new claim with a new excess applies.

## Can I insure more than one device?

Each Insure Plus device policy covers one device. To insure multiple devices, you need a separate policy for each one. You can hold multiple active device policies simultaneously on the platform.

## How quickly will my claim be processed?

Once all required documentation is submitted — including photographs of damage or a police report for theft — claims are assessed within 3 business days. Approved repairs are scheduled within 5 business days. Replacements are dispatched within 7 business days.

## Is my device covered if I lend it to someone?

Cover applies to the insured device regardless of who is using it, as long as the use is authorised by the policyholder. If someone uses your device without permission and damages it, the claim may be assessed differently depending on the circumstances.
