# Product Comparison Overview

> Insure Plus offers four insurance products — motor, bike, life, and device — each designed for a specific coverage need.

## Motor Insurance

Motor insurance covers your car against damage, theft, and third-party liability. It is legally required to drive on public roads. Insure Plus motor policies offer three coverage tiers: third-party only, third-party fire and theft, and comprehensive. Motor insurance requires 7 details to quote including vehicle registration and no-claims discount years.

## Bike Insurance

Bike insurance covers motorcycles and mopeds. Like motor, it is a legal requirement. Coverage tiers are the same three options as motor. Bike insurance is tailored to rider licence type and engine size. It requires 6 details to quote. Premiums tend to be lower than motor for equivalent coverage tiers.

## Life Insurance

Life insurance provides a lump-sum payment to your beneficiaries if you pass away during the policy term. Insure Plus life policies are term-based, covering periods from 5 to 30 years. Optional critical illness cover pays out if you are diagnosed with a specified serious condition. Life insurance requires 5 details to quote.

## Device Insurance

Device insurance covers smartphones, tablets, and laptops against accidental damage and theft. It does not require a vehicle licence or age minimum. Coverage is based on the declared device value. Device insurance requires 5 details to quote and is the quickest product to purchase on Insure Plus.

## Choosing the Right Product

If you are unsure which product you need, tell the assistant what you want to protect and it will recommend the right product. You can only have one active quote per product at a time.
