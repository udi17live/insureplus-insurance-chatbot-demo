# Payment and Policy Process

> Insure Plus processes payments securely through Stripe and issues your policy document automatically once payment is confirmed.

## How Payment Works

Insure Plus uses Stripe for all card payments. When you accept a quote, the assistant initiates a secure payment step directly in the chat. You enter your card details into a Stripe-hosted element — your card number never passes through Insure Plus servers. Payments are processed in real time.

## Accepted Payment Methods

Insure Plus accepts all major debit and credit cards including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Apple Pay and Google Pay are supported where available in your browser. Bank transfer and instalment plans are not currently available.

## What Happens After Payment

Once Stripe confirms your payment, your policy is created immediately. A policy number in the format INS-YEAR-PREFIX-XXXXXX is assigned. Your full policy document is emailed to the address on your account within a few minutes. The assistant will also display your policy number in chat.

## Payment Failures

If your card is declined, the assistant will inform you and ask you to try again. Your quote remains active and no charge is made. You can retry with the same card or use a different one. After three consecutive failures, the assistant advises you to contact your bank.

## Refunds and Cancellations

Policies can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase for a full refund, provided no claim has been made. To request a cancellation, contact Insure Plus support. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
