# Life Insurance Overview

> Insure Plus life insurance pays a lump sum to your beneficiaries if you pass away during the policy term, providing financial protection for those who depend on you.

## What Life Insurance Covers

Life insurance pays the sum assured — a fixed lump sum — to your named beneficiary if you die during the policy term. It does not pay out if you survive the policy term. The payout is tax-free and can be used by your beneficiary for any purpose, such as replacing lost income or paying off a mortgage.

## Policy Term

Insure Plus life insurance policies run for terms of 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, or 30 years. You choose the term when purchasing. The policy ends when the term expires. If you outlive the policy, no payment is made and no refund of premiums is provided.

## Sum Assured Options

The sum assured is the amount paid on a valid claim. Insure Plus offers fixed sum assured amounts of £50,000, £100,000, £150,000, £200,000, £300,000, and £500,000. You select your preferred amount during quoting.

## Critical Illness Option

You can add critical illness cover to your life policy. This pays the sum assured early if you are diagnosed with one of the specified critical illnesses during the term, rather than waiting until death. It does not provide a separate payment — the same sum assured is paid once for either event.

## Getting a Life Quote

The assistant needs your date of birth, smoking status, preferred sum assured, policy term in years, and whether you want critical illness cover. Five details in total.
