# Motor Insurance FAQ

> Answers to the most common questions about Insure Plus motor insurance.

## Can I add another driver to my policy?

Yes. You can add additional named drivers during the quote process. Each additional driver must be aged 18 or over with a valid UK licence. Adding a young or inexperienced driver may increase your premium.

## What is the minimum coverage I legally need?

In the UK, third-party only is the minimum legal requirement to drive on public roads. It covers injury or damage you cause to others but does not cover your own vehicle. Insure Plus offers third-party only as the entry-level coverage tier.

## Does my policy cover driving abroad?

Standard Insure Plus motor policies provide the legal minimum coverage required in EU countries while abroad. Comprehensive cover does not automatically extend abroad. If you need full comprehensive cover outside the UK, contact support before travelling.

## What happens if I sell my car mid-policy?

If you sell your vehicle, your policy becomes inactive. You can contact Insure Plus support to transfer the policy to a new vehicle or arrange a pro-rata refund for the remaining months if you do not need a replacement policy.

## Will my premium go up after a claim?

If you make a claim, your NCD resets to zero at renewal unless you have protected NCD. Your renewal premium may increase as a result. The exact impact depends on the nature of the claim and your renewal risk assessment.
