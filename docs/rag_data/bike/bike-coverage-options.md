# Bike Coverage Options

> Insure Plus bike insurance offers three coverage tiers — choose the level of protection that matches your needs and budget.

## Third-Party Only

Third-party only is the minimum legal coverage required to ride on public roads. It pays for injury or property damage you cause to other people. It does not cover damage to your own bike or theft. This is the lowest-cost option and is suitable for older bikes with low market value.

## Third-Party Fire and Theft

Third-party fire and theft includes everything in the third-party only tier, plus it covers your bike if it is stolen or damaged by fire. This tier is popular for bikes of moderate value where the owner wants theft protection without paying for full comprehensive cover.

## Comprehensive

Comprehensive is the highest tier and provides the widest protection. In addition to third-party liability and fire and theft, it covers damage to your own bike in an accident regardless of who was at fault. It also covers accidental damage while your bike is parked.

## What All Tiers Include

All three tiers include liability cover up to £20 million for third-party injury claims and up to £1 million for third-party property damage. All tiers are valid for riding in the UK.

## Choosing a Tier

For low-value bikes, third-party only may be sufficient. For bikes worth over £1,500, comprehensive cover is usually more cost-effective than paying out of pocket for repairs or replacement.
