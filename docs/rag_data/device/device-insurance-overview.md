# Device Insurance Overview

> Insure Plus device insurance covers smartphones, tablets, and laptops against accidental damage and theft, with instant cover from the moment of purchase.

## What Device Insurance Covers

Device insurance provides financial protection if your device is accidentally damaged or stolen. Accidental damage includes cracked screens, liquid damage, and physical breakage caused by drops. Theft cover applies when the device is taken without your consent. Cover applies in the UK and while travelling abroad.

## Eligible Devices

Insure Plus device insurance covers smartphones, tablets, and laptops. The device must be less than 3 years old at the time of purchase and in full working condition. Devices purchased second-hand are eligible provided the declared value reflects the current market value.

## Coverage Basis

Device insurance pays out based on the declared device value you provide during quoting. Payouts cover repair costs up to the declared value, or replacement with a device of equivalent specification if repair is not possible.

## Policy Duration

Device insurance policies run for 12 months. There is no automatic renewal — you will receive a reminder 30 days before expiry. You can renew or purchase a new policy through the assistant.

## Getting a Device Quote

The assistant needs your device type, make, model, declared value, and preferred coverage type. Five details in total. Device insurance is the quickest product to quote and purchase on the platform.
