# Motor Insurance Exclusions

> Insure Plus motor insurance does not cover certain situations — understanding these exclusions helps you avoid unexpected claim rejections.

## Driving Without a Valid Licence

Claims are not paid if the driver was operating the vehicle without a valid driving licence at the time of the incident. This includes a licence that has expired, been suspended, or was never obtained. The policy is void for that incident regardless of coverage tier.

## Driving Under the Influence

Any claim arising from an incident where the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs is excluded. This applies to both illegal substances and prescription medication if the driver was warned not to operate machinery.

## Use for Excluded Purposes

Motor policies cover private use and commuting only. Claims arising from use of the vehicle for hire, reward, delivery services, or racing are excluded. If you use your vehicle for business purposes beyond commuting, you must declare this.

## Wear and Tear

Gradual deterioration, mechanical breakdown, and wear and tear are not covered. Motor insurance covers sudden, unforeseen events — not maintenance costs. Engine failure due to lack of oil or a worn clutch, for example, would not be covered.

## Unlisted Drivers

Only named drivers on the policy are covered. If someone not listed on the policy was driving at the time of the incident, the claim will be declined. You can add additional drivers at the point of purchase.
