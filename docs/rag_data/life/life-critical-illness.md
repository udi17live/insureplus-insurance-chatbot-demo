# Life Insurance Critical Illness Cover

> Critical illness cover pays the sum assured early if you are diagnosed with a specified serious condition during the policy term.

## What Critical Illness Cover Does

Critical illness cover accelerates the life insurance payout. If you are diagnosed with one of the covered conditions during the policy term and survive for at least 14 days after diagnosis, the full sum assured is paid to you directly. The policy then ends. It does not pay again on death.

## Covered Conditions

Insure Plus critical illness cover pays out for the following conditions: heart attack, stroke, cancer (excluding minor skin cancers), kidney failure, major organ transplant, multiple sclerosis, and total permanent disability. Diagnosis must be confirmed by a registered specialist.

## How It Relates to Life Cover

Critical illness cover is an add-on to a life insurance policy, not a standalone product. It shares the same sum assured as the life cover. You cannot have a higher critical illness sum assured than the life sum assured.

## What It Does Not Cover

The critical illness benefit does not cover conditions not listed above, nor does it cover conditions you were already diagnosed with before purchasing the policy. Pre-existing conditions are not covered.

## Cost of the Add-On

Adding critical illness cover costs a fixed £15 per month added to your life insurance premium. This addition is the same regardless of your sum assured or policy term.
