# Life Insurance Premium Calculation

> Life insurance premiums are based on age, smoking status, sum assured, and policy term — all fixed at the time of purchase for the duration of the policy.

## Age and Risk

The older you are when you purchase life insurance, the higher your premium. Risk increases with age because the statistical probability of a claim rises. Premiums are locked in at the age you are when the policy starts and do not increase during the term.

## Smoking Status

Smokers pay higher premiums than non-smokers because smoking significantly increases health risks. On Insure Plus, a smoker is defined as anyone who has smoked in the past 12 months, including e-cigarettes. Declaring false smoking status voids the policy.

## Sum Assured

A higher sum assured means a higher payout on a claim, which results in a higher premium. Premiums scale proportionally with the sum assured selected. Doubling the sum assured approximately doubles the premium.

## Policy Term

Longer policy terms generally result in higher premiums because the insurer is exposed to risk for a longer period. A 30-year term will cost more per year than a 10-year term for the same sum assured and age.

## Critical Illness Loading

Adding critical illness cover increases the premium. The loading is a fixed addition on top of the base life premium and does not vary based on health conditions beyond age and smoking status.
