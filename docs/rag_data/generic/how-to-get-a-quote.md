# How to Get a Quote

> Getting a quote on Insure Plus takes under two minutes — just tell the assistant which product you want and answer a few simple questions.

## Starting a Quote

To start, simply tell the assistant what you want to insure. You can say something like "I want a quote for my car" or "get me bike insurance." The assistant will confirm the product and begin collecting the required details. You do not need to fill in any forms.

## Providing Your Details

The assistant asks for one piece of information at a time. For motor insurance, it will ask for your vehicle registration, make, year, date of birth, annual mileage, coverage type, and no-claims discount years. For other products the fields are similar but fewer. Each question includes a short hint so you know exactly what to enter.

## Confirming Your Details

Once all details are collected, the assistant presents a full summary before generating a quote. You can correct any field at this stage. The assistant will not proceed to pricing until you confirm the summary is accurate.

## Receiving Your Quote

After confirmation, the assistant generates your quote instantly. The quote shows the annual premium, a monthly breakdown, and the coverage tier you selected. Quotes are valid for 30 minutes. If a quote expires, the assistant regenerates it automatically using the same details.

## Completing Your Purchase

If you are happy with the quote, say "proceed" or "buy." The assistant will guide you through a secure payment step. Once payment succeeds, your policy document is emailed to you within minutes.
