# Life Insurance Pricing

> Insure Plus life insurance premiums are fixed at purchase and determined by sum assured, policy term, age band, and smoking status.

## Base Monthly Premiums (Non-Smoker, 10-Year Term)

The following rates apply to non-smokers purchasing a 10-year term policy.

£50,000 sum assured: £8 per month.
£100,000 sum assured: £14 per month.
£150,000 sum assured: £20 per month.
£200,000 sum assured: £26 per month.
£300,000 sum assured: £38 per month.
£500,000 sum assured: £60 per month.

## Age Band Adjustments (Monthly Addition)

Ages 18–29: no addition.
Ages 30–39: add £4 per month.
Ages 40–49: add £12 per month.
Ages 50–59: add £25 per month.

## Smoker Loading

Smokers pay an additional £10 per month on top of the age-adjusted premium, regardless of sum assured.

## Policy Term Adjustments (Multiplier Applied to Base)

10-year term: base rate (multiplier 1.0).
15-year term: multiplier 1.2.
20-year term: multiplier 1.4.
25-year term: multiplier 1.65.
30-year term: multiplier 1.9.

## Critical Illness Add-On

Adding critical illness cover adds £15 per month to the final premium, regardless of sum assured or term.

## Example Quotes

A 32-year-old non-smoker, £100,000 sum assured, 20-year term, no critical illness: (£14 + £4) × 1.4 = £25.20 per month. A 45-year-old smoker, £200,000 sum assured, 15-year term, with critical illness: (£26 + £12 + £10) × 1.2 + £15 = £72.60 per month.
