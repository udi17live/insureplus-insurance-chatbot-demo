# Device Insurance Claim Limits

> Device insurance claims are capped at the declared device value, with a per-claim excess and a maximum of two claims per policy year.

## Declared Value as the Claim Ceiling

The maximum payout for any single claim is the declared device value you provided when purchasing the policy. If you declare a device value of £600, no single claim can exceed £600. If your repair costs more, you pay the difference.

## Per-Claim Excess

Every claim is subject to a fixed excess of £50. The excess is deducted from the claim payout. For example, a £250 repair on a device declared at £800 results in a payout of £200.

## Maximum Two Claims Per Year

You can make a maximum of two claims in any 12-month policy period. After two claims, no further claims are accepted until the policy renews. This limit applies regardless of the nature of the incidents.

## No Claims Do Not Reduce Future Premiums

Unlike motor insurance, device insurance does not operate a no-claims discount system. Making zero claims does not reduce your renewal premium. Premiums are fixed based on the coverage tier and declared value.

## Replacement Value Basis

If a device is replaced rather than repaired, the replacement is based on the current market value of an equivalent device at the time of the claim, capped at the declared value. You will not receive a brand-new device if an equivalent refurbished model is available.
