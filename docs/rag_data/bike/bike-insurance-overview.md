# Bike Insurance Overview

> Insure Plus bike insurance covers motorcycles and mopeds against damage, theft, and third-party liability, with coverage options matched to rider licence type.

## What Bike Insurance Covers

Bike insurance provides financial protection if your motorcycle or moped is involved in an accident, stolen, or damaged by fire. Like motor insurance, it is a legal requirement to ride on public roads. Third-party only, third-party fire and theft, and comprehensive tiers are available.

## Who Can Get Bike Insurance

Bike insurance is available to riders aged 17 and over. The rider must hold a valid UK motorcycle licence appropriate for the engine size being ridden. AM licence holders are restricted to mopeds under 50cc. A1 licence covers up to 125cc. A2 and full A licences cover larger engine sizes.

## Coverage Tiers

Third-party only covers injury and damage caused to others. Third-party fire and theft adds protection if your bike is stolen or damaged by fire. Comprehensive covers all of the above plus damage to your own bike in an accident, regardless of fault.

## Policy Duration

All bike insurance policies run for 12 months. A renewal reminder is sent 30 days before expiry. You can renew through the assistant using your existing details.

## Getting a Bike Quote

The assistant needs your bike make, model, engine size in CC, your date of birth, licence type, and preferred coverage type. Six details in total. Quotes are generated instantly after confirmation.
