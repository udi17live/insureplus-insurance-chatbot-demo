# Bike Insurance FAQ

> Answers to the most common questions about Insure Plus bike insurance.

## Can I insure a scooter or moped?

Yes. Insure Plus covers mopeds and scooters up to 50cc under the AM licence category, as well as larger scooters under A1 or A2 licences depending on engine size. The make and engine CC are required during quoting.

## Is my bike covered if someone else rides it?

Only the named policyholder is covered unless additional riders are added during purchase. The assistant will ask if you want to add other riders. Each additional rider must hold a valid licence for the bike's engine size.

## Am I covered while pushing my bike?

If your bike breaks down and you are pushing it on the road, third-party liability cover applies. Comprehensive cover does not extend to damage sustained while the bike is being manually moved.

## Does comprehensive cover include helmet and leathers?

No. Helmet, leathers, and riding gear are not covered under any standard Insure Plus bike policy. These items require separate personal equipment cover, which is not currently available on the platform.

## What engine sizes can I insure?

Insure Plus covers bikes from 50cc mopeds up to 1,200cc motorcycles. Bikes with engine sizes above 1,200cc are not currently available for quoting.
