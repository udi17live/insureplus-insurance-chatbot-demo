# Bike Insurance Pricing

> Insure Plus bike insurance uses fixed annual premiums based on coverage tier and engine size, with a small adjustment for rider age.

## Base Annual Premiums by Coverage Tier

The following base rates apply to riders aged 25–39 on bikes up to 125cc.

Third-party only: £180 per year.
Third-party fire and theft: £250 per year.
Comprehensive: £340 per year.

## Engine Size Adjustments

Bikes up to 125cc pay the base rate. Bikes from 126cc to 500cc have £60 added. Bikes from 501cc to 1,000cc have £120 added. Bikes above 1,000cc have £180 added.

## Age Adjustments

Riders aged 17–20 have £120 added to the premium. Riders aged 21–24 have £60 added. Riders aged 25–39 pay the base rate. Riders aged 40 and over receive a £20 reduction.

## Example Quotes

A 22-year-old rider on a 125cc bike choosing comprehensive cover: £340 + £60 = £400 per year (£33.33 per month). A 30-year-old rider on a 600cc bike choosing third-party fire and theft: £250 + £120 = £370 per year (£30.83 per month).

## No Mileage Factor

Bike insurance premiums do not include a mileage adjustment. Mileage is not collected during the bike quoting process.
