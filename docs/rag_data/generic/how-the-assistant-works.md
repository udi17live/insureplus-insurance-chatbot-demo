# How the Insure Plus Assistant Works

> Insure Plus uses an AI assistant to guide you through getting a quote, comparing products, and purchasing a policy — all within a single conversation.

## What the Assistant Can Do

The Insure Plus assistant can answer questions about insurance products, collect the details needed for a quote, present pricing, and guide you through payment. It handles motor, bike, life, and device insurance. You can ask it anything about coverage, exclusions, pricing, or claims in plain language.

## How a Conversation Works

You start by telling the assistant what you need — for example, "I want motor insurance." The assistant then asks for the required details one at a time, such as your vehicle registration or date of birth. Once all details are collected, it presents a summary for your confirmation before generating a quote.

## Quote and Purchase Flow

After you confirm your details, the assistant generates a quote instantly. Quotes are valid for 30 minutes. If you are happy with the quote, the assistant initiates payment through a secure checkout. Once payment is confirmed, your policy is issued immediately and sent to your email.

## What the Assistant Cannot Do

The assistant cannot modify an existing policy, process a claim, or access your account details. For post-purchase support, please contact the Insure Plus support team directly. The assistant will always tell you when something is outside its scope.

## Privacy and Data Handling

Details you provide during a conversation are used only to generate your quote and policy. They are not stored beyond what is needed to complete your purchase. The assistant does not share your data with third parties outside of the policy issuance process.
