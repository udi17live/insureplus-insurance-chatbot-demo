# Bike Rider Eligibility

> Insure Plus bike insurance is available to riders aged 17 and over with a valid UK motorcycle licence appropriate for the engine size of the bike being insured.

## Minimum Age

The minimum age to purchase bike insurance on Insure Plus is 17. Riders aged 17–20 may attract higher premiums due to statistically higher claim rates among newer riders. There is no maximum age limit.

## Licence Types and Engine Restrictions

The AM licence permits riding mopeds up to 50cc with a maximum speed of 45 km/h. The A1 licence covers motorcycles up to 125cc. The A2 licence permits bikes up to 35 kW. The full A licence has no engine size restrictions and is required for bikes above 35 kW. The licence type you provide during quoting determines what engine sizes are insurable.

## Learner Riders

Riders on a Learner (L) plate with a CBT certificate can be quoted on bikes up to 125cc under the A1 category. The policy will note the learner status. Supervision requirements apply in accordance with UK law.

## Disqualified Drivers

Riders who are currently disqualified from driving or riding are not eligible for bike insurance on Insure Plus. A spent conviction that was not a disqualification does not affect eligibility.

## Residency Requirement

The policyholder must be a UK resident. Non-UK residents visiting the UK are not eligible for an Insure Plus bike policy.
